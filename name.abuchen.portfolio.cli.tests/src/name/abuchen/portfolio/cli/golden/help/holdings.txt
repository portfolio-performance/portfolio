pp-cli holdings: What is held at a date and what it is worth: holdings aggregated across investment accounts, or per account, with cash balances in a separate section, in the base currency or --currency.

Usage: pp-cli holdings [options]

Options:
  --date <yyyy-MM-dd>  Day to value the holdings at. Defaults to today. Holdings use the prices on or before this day, and conversions the rates on or before it.
  --investment-account <ref>  Only the holdings of this investment account. The cash section is not affected.
  --by-account  Split the holdings into one block per investment account instead of one aggregate. (switch, default off)
  --include-zero  Also list instruments held before the date and fully sold by it, with zero shares. They are hidden by default. (switch, default off)
  --currency <code>  ISO currency of the totals and converted amounts. Defaults to the base currency of the file. Amounts stay in their native currency as well.
  Global options (pp-cli --help): --file <path>, --format <format> (json), --verbose

Response data: What is held at the date.
  holdings (array of object) One block of holdings: the aggregate, or one block per investment account.
    investmentAccount (object, optional) The investment account of the block. Absent for the aggregate of all accounts.
      uuid (string) Canonical UUID.
      name (string) Account name; empty if the file has none.
    items (array of object) The holdings, ordered by name then UUID.
      instrument (string) UUID of the instrument.
      name (string) Instrument name; empty if the file has none.
      shares (number) Number of shares held.
      price (object, optional) The price valuation used: the last one on or before the date, or the last transaction's price if there is none. Absent for a sold-out holding.
        date (string) ISO date of the price.
        value (number) Price per share in the currency of the instrument. The currency may be a minor currency unit, such as ILA (1/100 ILS) or GBX (1/100 GBP); the figure is then in that unit. The value in the reporting currency is in converted.
      amount (number) Market value in the currency of the instrument. Minor units: as above.
      currencyCode (string) ISO currency code of the instrument. Minor units: as above.
      converted (object, optional) The market value in the reporting currency, at the rate of the date. Absent if no rate was found.
        currencyCode (string) The ISO currency code asked for.
        amount (number) The amount converted, rounded to minor units like core does.
        rate (number) Unrounded factor from the native to the converted currency.
        rateDate (string) ISO date of the rate; of the oldest if chained.
        rateSource (string, one of identity, transaction, instrument, ecb, fixed) Where the rate comes from: transaction is the record's own forex unit or transfer rate, instrument an exchange-rate instrument of the file, ecb the desktop app's ECB cache, fixed one of core's fixed pairs such as GBX to GBP.
    total (object, optional) Value of the block in the reporting currency. Absent if a holding has no rate.
      currencyCode (string) The reporting currency.
      amount (number) Sum of the converted amounts.
  cash (object) The cash accounts, whatever the investment-account filter.
    items (array of object) The balances, ordered by name then UUID.
      cashAccount (string) UUID of the cash account.
      name (string) Account name; empty if the file has none.
      amount (number) Balance at the date in the currency of the account.
      currencyCode (string) ISO currency code of the account.
      converted (object, optional) The balance in the reporting currency, at the rate of the date. Absent if no rate was found.
        currencyCode (string) The ISO currency code asked for.
        amount (number) The amount converted, rounded to minor units like core does.
        rate (number) Unrounded factor from the native to the converted currency.
        rateDate (string) ISO date of the rate; of the oldest if chained.
        rateSource (string, one of identity, transaction, instrument, ecb, fixed) Where the rate comes from: transaction is the record's own forex unit or transfer rate, instrument an exchange-rate instrument of the file, ecb the desktop app's ECB cache, fixed one of core's fixed pairs such as GBX to GBP.
    total (object, optional) Sum of the balances in the reporting currency. Absent if a balance has no rate.
      currencyCode (string) The reporting currency.
      amount (number) Sum of the converted amounts.

Response meta: fileVersion, asOf, date, currency; optional: investmentAccount, exchangeRates

Warnings:
  stale-price  The latest price is more than 7 days old. Not emitted for retired instruments, instruments with a manual quote feed or instruments without a price.
  exchange-rate-missing  No usable exchange rate for a currency pair; its figures are left out. Carries baseCurrency, termCurrency and the count of affected items.
  stale-exchange-rate  An exchange rate over 7 days older than the day needed was used. Carries baseCurrency, termCurrency, the count of records and maxAgeDays.
  exchange-rate-cache-unavailable  The desktop app's ECB rate cache is missing, unreadable or invalid, so it is not used. Carries the path if known and a reason: not-configured, not-found, unreadable or unparsable.

Error codes (meanings: pp-cli --help): unexpected-argument, unknown-option, option-value-required, invalid-option-value, file-required, file-not-found, file-unreadable, file-version-unsupported, password-required, incorrect-password, reference-not-found, reference-ambiguous, internal-error
